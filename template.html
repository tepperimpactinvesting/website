Tepper Impact Investing Club

Rethink Capital. Redefine Impact.

Founded Fall 2026. We give CMU students the tools, community and hands-on experience to put capital to work for impact.

Our purpose

Our purpose is to inspire and equip students to critically explore how finance and entrepreneurship can serve society, and to foster an active community dedicated to ethical, sustainable capital practices.

Our mission

Our mission closely aligns with the Tepper School's commitment to analytical leadership and using business to create meaningful impact. By encouraging students to critically examine how finance and entrepreneurship can address societal challenges, the club helps prepare future leaders to make informed, ethical decisions.

Member Program

Member Program.

Designed to provide an interdisciplinary perspective on impact investing, our Member Program combines foundational education, industry insights, and hands-on experiences to explore how finance and entrepreneurship can drive positive social and environmental change. To achieve active membership, prospective members must complete:

3

Lectures

Build foundational knowledge of impact investing, sustainable finance, and ethical decision-making.

3

Speaker Sessions

Gain insights from industry professionals and explore real-world applications of impact investing.

3

Hands-On Simulations or Volunteering Activities

Apply concepts through interactive exercises, collaborative experiences, and community engagement.
